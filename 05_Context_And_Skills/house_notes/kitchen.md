# Kitchen

- **Coffee machine:** fill the tank at the back, press the left button for a small cup and the right one for a large
  cup. The beans are in the tin marked *tea*.
- **Oven:** it runs about 20 degrees hotter than the dial says. For 200 °C, set 180.
- **Dishwasher:** tablets are in the drawer under the sink. Use the 50-minute programme, and open the door when it
  ends or the glasses stay wet.
- Eat anything in the fridge except the jar with the red lid.
