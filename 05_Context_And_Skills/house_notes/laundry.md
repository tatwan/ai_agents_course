# Laundry

- The washing machine is in the bathroom. Programme 3 (40 °C, one hour) is right for almost everything.
- Powder goes in the left drawer, one scoop.
- There is no dryer. The drying rack is behind the bedroom door, and clothes dry overnight next to the radiator.
- Do not run the machine after 10 at night. The neighbours below can hear the spin.
