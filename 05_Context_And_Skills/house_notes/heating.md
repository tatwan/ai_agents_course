# Heating and hot water

- The boiler is in the cupboard next to the bathroom. The thermostat is in the hall, set to 20 °C. Please leave it
  there.
- **No hot water:** look at the gauge on the front of the boiler. Below 1 bar, open the black tap under the boiler
  until the needle reaches 1.5, then close it and press the reset button once.
- **Radiators cold at the top:** leave them. We bleed them when we are back.
- The heating switches itself off from 11 at night to 6 in the morning.
