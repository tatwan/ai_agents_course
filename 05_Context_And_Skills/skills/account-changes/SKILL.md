# Account changes

An account is one email plus one customer record.

**Changing the email**

1. A customer can change their email **once per calendar year**. If they already changed it this year, say no and
   say when they can change it again (from 1 January).
2. Invoices already issued keep the old email as a snapshot. Downloads are tied to the email on the invoice.

**Deleting the account**

1. We remove the login and the marketing flag. Playlists are deleted with the account.
2. Invoices stay (kept 7 years, for tax). Support tickets stay for 24 months, then they are deleted.

Your reply says what changes now, what stays, and anything the customer cannot do.
