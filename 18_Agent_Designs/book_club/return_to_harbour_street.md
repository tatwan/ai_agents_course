# Return to Harbour Street

A novel by Jonah Abernathy. Paperback, 304 pages, 36 short chapters.

The Morales family is back. Two years after the events of *Harbour Street*, Rosa has finally opened the bicycle
repair shop she dreamed of, her brother Tiago has come home from the city with a secret, and their mother is
planning a seventieth birthday party that nobody is allowed to talk about. When a developer offers to buy every
house on the street, the family and their neighbours have to decide what home is worth.

Abernathy's second visit to Harbour Street keeps everything readers loved about the first: the arguments around the
kitchen table, the street football matches, the neighbour who reports every piece of gossip from her balcony. The
chapters are short, many of them only five or six pages, and each ends with a small surprise.

This is the second book in the Harbour Street series and continues directly from the first. Many of the jokes and
most of the family history depend on knowing what happened in *Harbour Street*, and the author's note at the front
recommends reading the books in order. New readers may find the opening chapters confusing, as several characters
are introduced only by their first names and their past is not explained again.

The ending is happy: the street stays, Tiago's secret turns out to be good news, and the birthday party is the best
the street has ever seen. Fans of the first book will close this one smiling.

A third book is planned for next year.
